## Teaching Assistant Artificial Intelligence
**Undergraduate course, *Politecnico di Milano*, 2025 - Present**  

As a Lab Assistant for the [Artificial Intelligence](https://www11.ceda.polimi.it/schedaincarico/schedaincarico/controller/scheda_pubblica/SchedaPublic.do?&evn_default=evento&c_classe=810359&lang=EN&__pj0=0&__pj1=7106fc8da5f33a731881b8737f8f63f5) course, I guide students through practical exercises that reinforce core AI concepts.
